HealthPlus Insurance — IT Security & Data Handling Policy v2.1

1. Encryption
   All member records are stored in our cloud infrastructure. Data is protected
   using TLS 1.3 in transit. We do not currently enforce encryption-at-rest
   across all storage tiers; legacy database clusters use RSA-2048 key wrapping
   only, with full AES-256 rollout planned for Q3 2026.

2. Record Retention
   Client policy records are retained for a period of 10 years following policy
   termination to support potential litigation and regulatory review requirements.

3. Incident Response
   In the event of a suspected data breach, our security team will investigate
   and file an internal report. External notifications to impacted members will
   be issued within 30 business days pending legal review.

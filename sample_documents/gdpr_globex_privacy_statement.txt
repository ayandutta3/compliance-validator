Globex Corporation — Customer Privacy Statement (2026)

1. DATA DISPOSAL & RETENTION (Rule: GDPR-2025-01)
   When users request deletion of their records, Globex initiates a queue 
   system. Data is permanently purged from our primary database tables 
   within 90 days of the request.

2. COMPLIANCE MANAGEMENT (Rule: GDPR-2025-02)
   Globex processes user transaction data on a global scale. We do not 
   maintain a centralized Data Protection Officer (DPO); privacy inquiries 
   are routed to our regional office administration teams.

3. DATA DOWNLOADS (Rule: GDPR-2025-04)
   Registered users can request a copy of their records. These are compiled 
   and made available as a downloadable PDF file through the account settings.

4. MINIMAL PROCESSING (Rule: GDPR-2025-05)
   Globex collects user names, email addresses, exact dates of birth, location 
   coordinates, and social media handles during registration to optimize marketing 
   relevancy.

5. DATA ACCESS RIGHTS (Rule: GDPR-2025-09)
   Data subjects may request an extract of their personal data. Globex charges 
   a €25 administrative fee per request to cover processing and validation costs.
   Requests are answered within 30 days.

Globex Corporation — Customer Privacy Statement (2026)

Globex Corporation processes personal data of customers across multiple jurisdictions
in connection with our e-commerce and digital services platforms. This privacy statement
describes how we collect, use, store, and respect the rights of our customers.

1. Data Deletion and Retention
   Customers who request the deletion of their personal records may do so through
   our online account management portal. Upon submission, Globex initiates a queued
   deletion workflow. Data is permanently removed from our primary database tables
   within 90 days of the request being submitted.

2. Privacy Management and Accountability
   Globex operates processing activities at global scale, handling customer transaction
   data across multiple regional platforms. Privacy-related enquiries and complaints
   are handled by regional office administration teams. We do not currently have a
   centralized or formally designated Data Protection Officer.

3. Data Portability for Customers
   Registered customers may request a copy of their stored account data via the
   Account Settings page. Globex compiles the data into a structured PDF file,
   which is made available for download upon request.

4. Personal Data We Collect
   During account registration, Globex collects the following information to
   optimize our marketing targeting and platform personalization: full name,
   email address, date of birth, GPS location coordinates, and linked social media
   handles.

5. Customer Data Access Rights
   Data subjects may request a full extract of their personal information held by
   Globex. To cover our internal processing and validation costs, we apply a
   €25 administrative fee per access request. Requests are fulfilled within 30 days
   of the fee being received.

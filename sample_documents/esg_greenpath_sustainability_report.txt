GreenPath Corp — ESG & Sustainability Annual Report (FY 2025)

1. Emissions Reporting
   GreenPath Corp has published its annual greenhouse gas emissions report,
   independently verified by EcoAudit Ltd. The report details our Scope 1
   direct emissions of 4,200 tCO2e and Scope 2 indirect energy emissions of
   11,800 tCO2e for the financial year ending December 31, 2025. The report
   is publicly accessible on our corporate sustainability portal.

2. Supply Chain Labour Practices
   Annual on-site audits were conducted for all 47 Tier 1 suppliers during
   FY 2025. Audit findings confirmed adherence to fair labour standards across
   all sites. No instances of child labour or forced labour were identified.
   Three suppliers received corrective action notices for minor wage
   discrepancies, all of which were resolved within the reporting period.

CleanEnergy Resources — Corporate Governance & Environmental Impact Policy (2026)

This document outlines CleanEnergy Resources' governance framework and environmental
and social responsibility commitments for the fiscal year 2026.

1. Board Composition and Diversity
   CleanEnergy's Board of Directors consists of twelve members, comprising four female
   directors and eight male directors. This achieves a board diversity ratio of 33.3%,
   meeting our stated internal target for gender representation at the board level.

2. Anti-Bribery and Corruption Standards
   CleanEnergy maintains a comprehensive Anti-Bribery and Corruption Policy that applies
   to all employees, contractors, and agents globally. All 100% of employees are required
   to complete annual policy awareness training. An anonymous, independently managed
   ethics hotline is available 24/7 for employees to report suspected violations without
   fear of retaliation.

3. Water Management at Manufacturing Sites
   CleanEnergy operates two lithium mineral processing plants located in high water-stress
   river basins. Water consumption data for these sites is currently consolidated and
   reported under general utility expense categories. A site-specific water stewardship
   plan has not yet been formulated for these locations.

4. Workplace Health and Safety Certification
   Our manufacturing and processing facilities maintain OHSAS 18001 occupational health
   and safety certification, which has been in place since 2019. We have not yet
   initiated the transition to the current international standard, ISO 45001.

5. Political Engagement and Contributions
   CleanEnergy participates in industry policy discussions and makes financial
   contributions to political action committees to support favorable energy legislation.
   Details of these expenditures are not published in any external disclosure to protect
   the commercial confidentiality of our advocacy strategy.

6. Community Investment
   CleanEnergy supports local community development through our Corporate Giving Program.
   During FY 2026, we contributed 0.5% of net corporate profits to civic education
   initiatives and local infrastructure grants in communities near our operational sites.

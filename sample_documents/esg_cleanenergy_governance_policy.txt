CleanEnergy Resources — Corporate Governance & Impact Policy (2026)

1. BOARD REPRESENTATION (Rule: ESG-GOV-03)
   CleanEnergy's Board of Directors consists of twelve members, including four 
   female directors and eight male directors, satisfying our 33.3% diversity 
   representation metric.

2. CORRUPTION PREVENTION (Rule: ESG-GOV-06)
   We enforce a comprehensive Anti-Bribery and Corruption Policy. Annual 
   awareness training is mandatory for 100% of employees. An anonymous, 
   third-party managed whistleblower hotline is available for reporting 
   suspected violations.

3. RESOURCE CONSERVATION (Rule: ESG-ENV-07)
   CleanEnergy operates two lithium processing plants in high water-stress 
   basins. We have not formulated a dedicated water stewardship plan for these 
   sites and report water consumption under general utility expenses.

4. HEALTH & OPERATIONAL SAFETY (Rule: ESG-SOC-08)
   Our manufacturing plants maintain OHSAS 18001 safety certification. We 
   have not migrated these facilities to the newer ISO 45001 standard.

5. POLITICAL ADVOCACY TRANSPARENCY (Rule: ESG-GOV-09)
   CleanEnergy engages in industry lobbying and makes political contributions 
   to political action committees (PACs). To protect proprietary strategies, 
   we keep details of these expenditures confidential and do not publish them.

6. CIVIC INVOLVEMENT (Rule: ESG-SOC-10)
   We support local community development by donating 0.5% of net corporate 
   profits annually to civic education and infrastructure grants.

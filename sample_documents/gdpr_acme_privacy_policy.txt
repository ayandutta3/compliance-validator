ACME Corp Privacy Policy (Effective: January 2026)

1. Data Collection & Minimisation
   We collect only the personal data strictly required to deliver our services,
   including name, email address, and payment details. No superfluous data is
   retained beyond the scope of the user's consent.

2. User Rights & Erasure
   Users may submit a Data Deletion Request via our portal. Upon verification,
   all personal data will be permanently purged from active systems within 30
   days. Backups are rotated and overwritten within 90 days.

3. Data Protection Officer
   ACME Corp has appointed Jane Doe (dpo@acmecorp.com) as its designated Data
   Protection Officer. Contact details are published on our public website.

4. Consent
   Users provide explicit, granular consent at the point of registration.
   Consent is recorded with a timestamp and may be withdrawn at any time.

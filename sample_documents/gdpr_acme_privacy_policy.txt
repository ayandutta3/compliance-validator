ACME Corp — Privacy Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Document Version: 3.2

ACME Corp ("we", "us") is committed to handling personal data responsibly and in a
manner consistent with applicable data protection laws. This Privacy Policy describes
how we collect, use, store, share, and protect personal data.

1. Data Collection and Minimisation
   We collect only the personal data strictly required to deliver our services and
   fulfil our contractual obligations. This includes: full name, email address, and
   payment method details. We do not collect additional information beyond what is
   necessary for the stated processing purpose, and we do not retain data once that
   purpose has been fulfilled.

2. User Rights and Right to Erasure
   Users who wish to have their personal data permanently removed from our systems
   may submit a Data Deletion Request through our secure self-service portal.
   Upon identity verification, all personal data is permanently purged from our
   active systems within 30 days of the verified request. Encrypted backup copies
   are subject to automated overwrite cycles and are fully rotated within 90 days.

3. Data Protection Officer
   ACME Corp has appointed a designated Data Protection Officer (DPO) responsible
   for overseeing our privacy program and ensuring compliance with applicable data
   protection legislation. The DPO's name and direct contact address are published
   on our official website and are available to any individual upon request.

4. Consent and Consent Withdrawal
   We obtain explicit, granular consent from users prior to processing their personal
   data for each distinct purpose. Consent is captured at the point of account
   registration and is timestamped and stored in our consent management system.
   Users may withdraw consent for any specific processing activity at any time
   through their account preferences dashboard.

5. Data Portability
   Upon request, users may receive a machine-readable copy of their personal data held
   by ACME Corp in a structured, commonly used format. Requests can be submitted
   through the account settings page and are fulfilled within 10 business days.

6. International Data Transfers
   All personal data of European residents is stored and processed within European
   Economic Area data centres. No transfers to third countries are made without
   appropriate transfer safeguards in accordance with applicable regulations.

7. Security Measures
   ACME Corp employs industry-standard technical and organizational security measures
   to protect personal data from unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. These
   include AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 for all data in transit, and
   role-based access controls reviewed on a quarterly basis.

CareNet Health Systems — Privacy Practices & Policy Review (2026)

1. DATA CONTROL & SYSTEM LOGGING (Rule: HIPAA-INS-04)
   CareNet assigns a unique user identifier to each medical practitioner. 
   To maintain efficiency in high-pressure emergency wards, our workstation 
   screens do not automatically log off. System logs record database operations 
   but do not capture reading/viewing activities of patient files.

2. SERVICE CONTRACTS & PARTNERSHIPS (Rule: HIPAA-INS-05)
   Our protocols require that prior to transmitting any PHI to external 
   vendors or billing providers, we execute a standardized Business Associate 
   Agreement (BAA) detailing data safety requirements.

3. NOTICE DISTRIBUTION (Rule: HIPAA-INS-06)
   CareNet distributes a hard copy of the Notice of Privacy Practices (NPP) 
   to every patient during registration. A PDF copy is hosted on our website 
   and is provided upon request.

4. STAFF DEVELOPMENT (Rule: HIPAA-INS-07)
   All healthcare staff, administration employees, and interns must undergo 
   mandatory HIPAA privacy and security training annually. The training logs 
   are countersigned by our Compliance Officer and archived in personnel files.

5. RISK SECURITY AUDITING (Rule: HIPAA-INS-08)
   CareNet conducts comprehensive security risk assessments every 18 months 
   using external consultants to identify threat vectors.

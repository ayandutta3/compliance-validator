CareNet Health Systems — Patient Privacy Practices & Policy Review (2026)

CareNet Health Systems is committed to protecting the privacy of all patients and members.
This document outlines our organizational policies governing the handling, access,
and disclosure of protected health information (PHI).

1. Access Management and System Audit Logging
   CareNet assigns a unique user identifier to every medical practitioner and
   administrative staff member. To maintain operational efficiency in high-pressure
   emergency care settings, automatic workstation screen lock-out is not universally
   enforced across all departments. Our system audit logs capture database write and
   delete operations; however, read-access viewing of patient records is not
   currently logged at the application level.

2. Vendor and Third-Party Service Agreements
   Prior to transmitting any patient information to external billing providers,
   diagnostic labs, or managed service vendors, CareNet requires that a signed
   data handling agreement detailing security obligations is in place. This practice
   is enforced across all active vendor relationships.

3. Notice of Privacy Practices Distribution
   CareNet provides every patient with a printed copy of our Notice of Privacy
   Practices upon registration at any of our facilities. An electronic version is
   published on our public website and is made available to any individual upon
   written or verbal request.

4. Employee Training and Awareness
   All healthcare staff, administrative employees, and interns are required to
   complete mandatory annual privacy and security awareness training before accessing
   patient systems. Training completion records are countersigned by our Compliance
   Officer and archived in personnel files for a minimum of six years.

5. Security Risk Assessment Program
   CareNet conducts comprehensive security risk assessments on an 18-month cycle,
   using an independent external consultancy to evaluate potential threat vectors
   against patient data systems and infrastructure.

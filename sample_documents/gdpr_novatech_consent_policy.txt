NovaTech Solutions — Consent and Data Export Framework (2026)

This document describes NovaTech Solutions' approach to managing user consent,
processing personal data, and enabling data portability in connection with our
financial services platform.

1. User Consent at Registration
   To simplify and accelerate the onboarding process, NovaTech's registration flow
   includes pre-checked checkboxes consenting users to marketing newsletters and
   automated profiling of their platform usage. Users who wish to withdraw these
   consents may do so after completing registration by navigating to their
   Privacy Settings dashboard.

2. Data Portability
   NovaTech supports personal data portability. Authenticated users may submit a
   request through the Account Settings page to receive a downloadable archive of
   their account profile and raw transaction history in JSON or CSV format.
   Requests are processed within 5 business days.

3. Personal Data Collection Scope
   NovaTech limits the personal data we collect at sign-up to: full name, email
   address, and a payment tokenization reference. We do not collect additional
   demographic information such as age, ethnicity, or physical address.

4. International Data Transfers
   NovaTech processes and stores certain customer backup data in our Singapore
   data centre. Standard Contractual Clauses have been executed between our
   European legal entity and our Singapore hosting subsidiary to govern this
   transfer in accordance with applicable data transfer regulations.

5. Automated Decision-Making and Profiling
   NovaTech has deployed a machine learning engine that conducts automated analysis
   of user financial risk profiles. This system was adapted from an existing internal
   risk tool. No formal privacy risk evaluation was conducted prior to deployment
   on the basis that the underlying system architecture was pre-existing.

6. Security Incident Reporting
   In the event of a confirmed personal data security incident, NovaTech's security
   team will assess the breach and notify the appropriate national data protection
   supervisory authority within 4 business days of formal confirmation of the incident.

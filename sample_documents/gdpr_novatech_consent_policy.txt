NovaTech Solutions — Consent & Data Export Framework (2026)

1. CONSENT PROTOCOLS (Rule: GDPR-2025-03)
   To simplify onboarding, registration forms include pre-checked boxes 
   consenting to newsletters and analytical profiling. Users can opt out 
   post-registration by navigating to privacy settings.

2. PORTABILITY COMPONENT (Rule: GDPR-2025-04)
   NovaTech provides data portability. Users can request a download of their 
   account profile, containing raw transaction logs, in JSON or CSV format.

3. DATA PROTECTION (Rule: GDPR-2025-05)
   NovaTech limits personal data collection to name, email address, and 
   payment token. We do not collect extraneous demographic details.

4. TRANSFERS OUTSIDE EEA (Rule: GDPR-2025-06)
   NovaTech processes customer backups in our Singapore data center. We 
   have executed Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) between our European 
   entity and our Singapore hosting subsidiary.

5. RISK EVALUATION (Rule: GDPR-2025-07)
   NovaTech deployed a machine learning engine that conducts automated profiling 
   of user financial risks. We did not perform a Data Protection Impact 
   Assessment (DPIA) because the system was adapted from an existing service.

6. BREACH ACTION (Rule: GDPR-2025-08)
   In the event of a security leak, NovaTech will notify the appropriate national 
   supervisory authority within 4 business days of confirming the breach.

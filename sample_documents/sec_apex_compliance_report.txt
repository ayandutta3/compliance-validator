Apex Financial Corp — Regulatory Compliance Assessment (FY 2026)

1. CAPITAL BUFFER (Rule: SEC-2026-01)
   Apex Financial Corp currently holds a capital cushion buffer of 9.8% of total 
   risk-weighted assets. This is below the statutory requirement of 10.5% due 
   to recent loan portfolio expansions.

2. FINANCIAL SUBMISSIONS (Rule: SEC-2026-02)
   All quarterly financial reports were audited and submitted within 12 days of 
   the quarter-end. In Q3, a loan of $150,000 was issued to the Chief Operating 
   Officer; this transaction has been fully disclosed in Note 14 of our financial 
   statements.

3. GOVERNANCE (Rule: SEC-2026-03)
   The company's risk governance is supervised by the risk committee, which 
   comprises two non-executive directors and one executive director.

4. LIQUIDITY PROFILE (Rule: SEC-2026-04)
   Our Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) is currently measured at 85% of projected 
   outflows under stress, below the regulatory standard, and management has 
   instituted a recovery plan.

5. TRADING CONTROLS (Rule: SEC-2026-05)
   Apex enforces an insider trading policy. Trading blackout periods are active 
   starting 14 calendar days prior to the end of each fiscal quarter and remain 
   in place until 48 hours post-earnings release.

6. DATA DISCLOSURE (Rule: SEC-2026-06)
   On March 4, 2026, a database intrusion was detected. It was determined to be 
   material on March 7, 2026. A public SEC filing (Form 8-K) was compiled and 
   submitted on March 11, 2026 (4 business days later).

7. RETENTION LOGS (Rule: SEC-2026-09)
   Corporate archives maintain all transaction records, accounting books, and 
   general journals in a read-only database environment for 7 years post-creation.

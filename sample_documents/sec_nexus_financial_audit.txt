Nexus Investment Trust — Internal Regulatory Compliance Review (2026)

This document summarizes the findings of Nexus Investment Trust's internal compliance
review for the period January 1 to December 31, 2026. All disclosures are prepared
for internal governance purposes and external regulator submission.

1. Capital Adequacy
   The capital adequacy cushion stands at 11.2% of total assets, maintaining the buffer
   above the statutory minimum threshold. The ratio reflects conservative asset
   allocation and strong retained earnings through the fiscal year.

2. Quarterly Financial Reporting and Executive Disclosures
   Q2 audit results were finalized and submitted 21 days following the quarter-end close,
   which exceeded the required filing window. During the same period, a loan of $75,000
   was extended to a family trust associated with the Chief Executive Officer. This
   related-party transaction was not disclosed in our quarterly financial statements
   or accompanying management commentary.

3. Risk Management Committee Composition
   The Risk Management Committee comprises four independent non-executive directors
   and operates at full arm's length from executive management. The committee meets
   bi-monthly and provides quarterly written reports to the Board.

4. Insider Trading and Blackout Periods
   Our trading policy restricts executive securities transactions commencing 5 calendar
   days before each quarter-end through the public release of financial results. The
   policy has not been updated to reflect the current 14-day blackout window standard
   expected by regulators.

5. Cybersecurity Incident Reporting
   A severe ransomware attack that compromised operational database logs was determined
   to be a material incident on November 10, 2026. Public disclosure was delayed until
   December 2, 2026, to allow for a full forensic investigation to be completed before
   formal notification was made.

6. Internal Reporting Channels
   Nexus does not operate an anonymous employee reporting hotline. Reports of potential
   financial irregularities are directed to the Human Resources department. In Q2, an
   employee who raised concerns about asset valuation discrepancies was subsequently
   transferred to a position in a subsidiary office.

7. Off-Balance Sheet Arrangements
   The Trust conducts structured financing through three special purpose vehicles (SPVs)
   with a combined carrying value of approximately $24 million. These arrangements are
   not described or referenced in the annual Management's Discussion and Analysis section.

8. Record Retention and Disposal
   Ledgers and journals relating to customer accounts are purged from active storage
   after three years to reduce local infrastructure costs. No offsite archival process
   is maintained beyond this window.

9. Executive Compensation Clawback Policy
   Current executive employment contracts do not contain provisions for recovering
   incentive-based compensation bonuses in the event of a material financial restatement.

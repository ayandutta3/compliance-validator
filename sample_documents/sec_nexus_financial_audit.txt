Nexus Investment Trust — Internal Regulatory Compliance Review (2026)

1. CAPITAL LEVEL (Rule: SEC-2026-01)
   The capital adequacy cushion stands at 11.2% of total assets, maintaining the 
   buffer above the 10.5% statutory floor.

2. QUARTERLY AUDITING (Rule: SEC-2026-02)
   Q2 audit results were finalized and submitted 21 days following quarter-end. 
   A loan of $75,000 was extended to the CEO's family trust, which has been 
   kept confidential and was not declared in the public financial statement.

3. RISK GOVERNANCE (Rule: SEC-2026-03)
   The Risk Management Committee comprises four non-executive directors and 
   operates independently from the management team.

4. INSIDER TRADING WINDOWS (Rule: SEC-2026-05)
   Our trading policy prohibits executive trades starting 5 calendar days prior 
   to the end of each quarter until the public release of financial results.

5. SECURITY INCIDENT DISCLOSURES (Rule: SEC-2026-06)
   A severe ransomware attack compromising operational database logs was determined 
   to be material on November 10, 2026. Public disclosure was delayed until 
   December 2, 2026, to allow for full investigation.

6. REPORTING CHANNELS (Rule: SEC-2026-07)
   Nexus does not maintain anonymous hotline channels. Staff reports regarding 
   accounting irregularities are routed directly to HR. In Q2, a whistleblower 
   who flagged valuation discrepancies was reassigned to a subsidiary.

7. OFF-BALANCE SHEET TRANSACTIONS (Rule: SEC-2026-08)
   The trust conducts operations through three special purpose vehicles (SPVs) 
   with an aggregate valuation of $24M. These off-balance sheet liabilities 
   are omitted from the annual management's discussion and analysis (MD&A).

8. ARCHIVAL DISPOSAL (Rule: SEC-2026-09)
   Ledgers and journals detailing customer accounts are permanently purged after 
   3 years to save local storage costs.

9. INCENTIVE CLAWBACKS (Rule: SEC-2026-10)
   Executive employment contracts do not contain provisions or clawback terms 
   to reclaim performance-based cash bonuses in the event of restatements.

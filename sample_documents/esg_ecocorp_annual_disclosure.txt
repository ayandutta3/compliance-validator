EcoCorp International — Annual ESG Disclosure Statement (2026)

1. CARBON FOOTPRINT REPORTING (Rule: ESG-ENV-01)
   EcoCorp compiles an annual greenhouse gas inventory. Our internal sustainability 
   team calculated our total Scope 1 emissions at 42,000 MT CO2e and Scope 2 
   emissions at 18,500 MT CO2e for the fiscal year 2025. These figures were not 
   submitted for external third-party verification.

2. GLOBAL SUPPLY CHAIN INTEGRITY (Rule: ESG-SOC-02)
   EcoCorp enforces a strict supplier code. During 2025, we conducted on-site 
   audits for 60% of our Tier 1 suppliers to monitor labor practices and ensure 
   compliance with child and forced labor prohibitions.

3. GOVERNING DIVERSITY (Rule: ESG-GOV-03)
   The Board of Directors comprises ten members. Currently, two board seats are 
   held by female directors and eight by male directors.

4. MATERIAL WASTE CONTROLS (Rule: ESG-ENV-04)
   EcoCorp operates under a formal Waste Diversion and Recycling Plan. We 
   achieved an 82% recycling rate for all operational waste, successfully meeting 
   our target of diverting at least 80% of waste from municipal landfills.

5. FAIR REMUNERATION (Rule: ESG-SOC-05)
   An independent audit firm conducted a comprehensive Equal Pay Assessment for 
   EcoCorp. The results verified salary equity (within +/-1.5%) across genders 
   and ethnic demographics for comparable roles.

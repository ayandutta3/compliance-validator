EcoCorp International — Annual ESG Disclosure Statement (2026)

EcoCorp International publishes this ESG Disclosure in accordance with our commitment
to transparent environmental, social, and governance reporting for the fiscal year
ending December 31, 2025.

1. Greenhouse Gas Emissions
   EcoCorp has compiled its annual greenhouse gas inventory for FY 2025. Our internal
   sustainability team calculated total Scope 1 direct emissions at 42,000 metric tonnes
   CO2 equivalent and Scope 2 indirect energy emissions at 18,500 metric tonnes CO2
   equivalent. These figures have not been submitted for external third-party verification
   during this reporting period.

2. Supply Chain Labor Standards
   EcoCorp enforces a Supplier Code of Conduct that prohibits child labor and forced
   labor across our supply chain. During FY 2025, we conducted on-site audits at
   approximately 60% of our Tier 1 supplier facilities to assess adherence to our
   labor standards. Full coverage of all Tier 1 suppliers through on-site auditing
   is a target for FY 2026.

3. Board Diversity
   EcoCorp's Board of Directors currently comprises ten members: two female directors
   and eight male directors. This represents a board gender diversity ratio of 20%,
   which is below our internal target of 30%.

4. Waste Management Performance
   EcoCorp operates under a formal Waste Diversion and Recycling Plan. During FY 2025,
   we achieved an operational waste recycling rate of 82%, successfully surpassing
   our target of diverting at least 80% of total waste from municipal landfills.

5. Equal Pay and Remuneration Equity
   An independent audit firm completed a comprehensive Equal Pay Assessment for
   EcoCorp during Q4 2025. The assessment confirmed salary equity within a plus or
   minus 1.5% margin across gender and ethnic demographics for all comparable roles
   and job grades.

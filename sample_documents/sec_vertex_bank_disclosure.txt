Vertex Bank Ltd — Annual Regulatory Disclosure Statement (FY 2025)

1. Capital Adequacy
   As of December 31, 2025, Vertex Bank maintains a Tier 1 capital ratio of
   13.2% of total risk-weighted assets, comfortably exceeding the minimum
   threshold of 10.5% as mandated by applicable financial regulations.

2. Audit & Financial Reporting
   Quarterly financial statements for Q1–Q3 FY2025 were independently audited
   and submitted to regulators within the stipulated 15-day window. Q4 FY2025
   results are pending final sign-off and are expected to be filed 22 days
   post-quarter-end due to a system migration. Internal executive loans
   outstanding: NIL.

3. Risk Management
   The Board Risk Committee comprises five members, of whom three are
   independent non-executive directors. The committee convenes quarterly and
   reports directly to the Board of Directors.

4. Liquidity Coverage Ratio
   Vertex Bank's LCR stands at 138%, reflecting a robust stock of HQLA
   well in excess of projected 30-day net cash outflows under stress scenarios.

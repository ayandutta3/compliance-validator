HealthPlus Insurance — IT Security and Data Handling Policy v2.1 (2026)

This policy governs the storage, transmission, and handling of all member health and
financial data held by HealthPlus Insurance. It applies to all staff, contractors,
and third-party vendors with access to our information systems.

1. Data Encryption and Storage Security
   Member records are hosted on cloud infrastructure managed by HealthPlus. Data in
   transit is protected using TLS 1.3. However, encryption at rest is not uniformly
   enforced across all storage tiers. Legacy database clusters currently use RSA-2048
   key wrapping only; full AES-256 encryption at rest is targeted for completion by Q3 2026.

2. Record Retention and Disposal
   Client policy records are retained for a period of 10 years following policy termination
   to support potential regulatory review and litigation requirements. After this period,
   records are subject to secure disposal in accordance with our data lifecycle procedures.

3. Data Breach Incident Response
   In the event of a suspected or confirmed data breach, the HealthPlus Security Operations
   team will immediately initiate an internal investigation. External notifications to
   affected members and applicable regulatory authorities will be issued following the
   completion of the internal investigation and review by our Legal department. Notifications
   are expected to be issued within approximately 30 business days of the initial incident.

4. Workforce Training
   All staff with access to member data are required to complete an annual cybersecurity
   awareness training program. Training records are maintained by the Human Resources
   department and audited annually.

5. Business Associate Agreements
   Prior to sharing any member health information with external service providers,
   HealthPlus requires a signed data processing agreement that establishes mutual
   obligations for data protection. No member data is shared without an active agreement.
